# Absolute Fresh — Premium South Indian Breakfast Brand Website

A premium product-first website for **Absolute Fresh Food** — focused on fresh Idli & Dosa Batter, freshly made chutneys, and breakfast combo packs.

Designed to feel like a real modern FMCG/D2C food brand rather than a generic startup landing page.

---

## Brand Positioning

> Fresh South Indian Breakfast. Ready in Minutes.

Absolute Fresh focuses on:
- Stone-ground batter
- Freshly made chutneys
- Small batch production
- Chilled local delivery
- WhatsApp-first ordering

---

# Website Philosophy

This website is intentionally designed to:

✅ Feel calm and premium  
✅ Focus heavily on products  
✅ Increase order conversion  
✅ Work beautifully on mobile  
✅ Support WhatsApp ordering  
✅ Feel like a real food brand

Inspired by modern Indian FMCG brands:
- iD Fresh
- Paper Boat
- Epigamia
