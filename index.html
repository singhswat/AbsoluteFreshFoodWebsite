Got Questions

Frequently asked questions

How long does the batter stay fresh?
Best consumed within 3–4 days of delivery when stored at 2°C–5°C (standard refrigerator). The natural fermentation continues slowly in the fridge, which slightly increases the sour note over time — many customers prefer day 2 or 3 for dosas. We never use preservatives to extend this shelf life artificially.
Why is stone-ground batter better than blender batter?
Stone grinding is slower and cooler than a blender. This preserves the cell structure of the grains, creating a coarser, more aerated batter. The result is idlis that are softer and more spongy, and dosas with a more distinct crunch. Blenders generate heat that affects the texture and fermentation quality.
Do I need to add anything to the batter before cooking?
The batter is ready to use straight from the jar. Just shake or stir gently before pouring. Some customers add a pinch of salt to taste — but add it only to the portion you're using, not the whole jar, as salt can slow fermentation. No water needs to be added for idlis. For dosas, you can thin slightly with a tablespoon of water for a crispier result.
